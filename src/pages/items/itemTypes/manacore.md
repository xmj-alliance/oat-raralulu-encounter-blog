# Manacore

- Name=Manacore
- Alias=Managen, Epsilon Reactor, The Power Core
- Type=Underwear

# Overview
Manacore is a collection of energy cores installable onto living humanoids.

It serves as energy source for Mana bracers and greaves, boosting wearer's strength and duality, allowing the wearer to cast Mana abilities through them.

It also creates energy crystal fabric underwear for the wearer.

# Appearance
Commonly, a Manacore looks like a metallic circle inlayed onto a cross-shaped base

It always emits light with various color. Light becomes brighter as output increases

Part of the output energy materializes as soft fabric-like texture, wrapping wearer's lower half, served as an underwear.

# Wearing
The core is installed on the body, at lower abdomen.

Once installed, it deeply integrates with the wearer.

Installation is easy and straight forward

Removal is painful and potentially dangerous without the help from the professionals.

# Qualities
from common low-end to rare high-end: 
Plain, Bronze, Silver, Gold, Platimum, Ultra

Generally, lower qualities like plain models are unstable.

Most Plain models require connecting wires to Mana bracers and greaves. Higher qualities do not require wires.

Higher quality models have higher stability, capacity and output rate. They support more Mana abilities.

# How it works
Manacore collects soul energy, and converts it into power and abilities.

## Abilities
- outputing energy to the body, boosting strength and duality
- powering Mana Bracers and Mana Greaves, supporting their abilities.

## Charging
The following are ways to charge a Manacore
- Touching another living creature with two bare hands or two bare feet 
  - to extract their soul energy
- Hijacking another Manacore with a bare hand or a bare feet and force the energy extraction
- Connecting to another Manacore with wire. (If wires are supported)

## Low capacity
When fully charged, the wearer feels energetic.

When nearly drained, wearer feels anxious and hungry.

Leaving the core depleted is dangerous, as the core starts to feed on wearer's soul energy after deletion.

Higher the energy output, higher the consumption rate, greater the risk while depleted.

# Availability
Common models like Plain and Bronze and some rare Silver models are sold by merchants.

Silver models and very rare models like Gold and Platimum are prizes in the tournements.

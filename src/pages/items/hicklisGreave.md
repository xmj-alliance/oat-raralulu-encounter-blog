# Hicklis Greave
- Name=Hicklis Greave
- Type=Mana Greave

## Appearance
Gilded greave emiting golden light.

Having detailed markings describing an arena scene
- a gladiator winning the combat and conquering his opponent.

## Abilities
- Kinetic barrier
  - Blocks or dampens all significant incoming movements toward around the greave
  - Whitelists creatures or objects being stepped on by the wearer
- Super jump
- Power kick

## Requirements
Manacore of Gold tier and above.

## Stages

### 10
This is the only semester for this academic year of the College of Winterhold. The 4 (5) new faces are showing up in their first class: J'zargo, Brelyna, Onmund, Dovahkiin(, ???). Walking along the ancient corridor, decorated with antique sculptures and paintings, the new students feel the air of magic gently flowing through them. The corridor itself seems to hum with latent power, and whispers of long-gone mages echo softly.

They arrive at the Hall of the Elements, a grand chamber with high ceilings and glowing runes etched into the walls. They see Tolfdir, the master wizard, standing at the center, surrounded by mystical artifacts, expecting them and ready for a warm welcome.

### 20
Tolfdir introduces himself with a warm smile.

Afterward, the students introduce themselves one by one.

### 30
Tolfdir presents an introduction to magic, explaining the different schools of thought and practice within the arcane arts. He emphasizes the importance of understanding the basics before attempting more advanced spells.

### 40 An improvised prank of J'zargo
J'zargo, with a mischievous grin, decides to show off his "superior" skills.

Possible pranks include:
- Casting a small, harmless jolt of electricity at Onmund, causing him to yelp in surprise and everyone else to laugh.
- Creating a dazzling display by casting a fire cloak full of lights and aura around himself, momentarily blinding the room with its brilliance.
- Making the floor slippery by casting water and frost spells, causing Tolfdir to slip and fall, which might lead to a serious conversation about respect and responsibility.
- (Or other pranks)

### 60 Safety first!
With J'zargo's prank causing a bit of chaos, Tolfdir sternly but kindly explains the potential dangers caused by the misuse of magic and emphasizes the principle of safety first.

He shares a cautionary tale of one of his past students who let his ambition outpace his caution.

To reinforce this lesson, Tolfdir introduces the concept of Ward magic, which is used to protect oneself and others from magical harm. He demonstrates the spell and explains its practical applications in both combat and everyday life.

### 80
Ward magic Presentation session. Tolfdir encourages Dovahkiin to try out the Ward magic, even if it is his first time doing so.

To continue the demonstration, Tolfdir asks Dovahkiin to keep the ward active, then casts a fireball at Dovahkiin. Dovahkiin tries to absorb the fireball with the ward. 

### 100 - 🙌
Class is over. Tolfdir makes a concluding speech, summarizing the key points of the lesson and expressing his confidence in their potential.

Finally, Tolfdir reminds everyone about the field study at Saarthal scheduled for the next day, emphasizing the importance of punctuality and preparation.

Mission accomplished!

### 120 - ❌
Any professor or student is dead in an accident. Mission Failed.

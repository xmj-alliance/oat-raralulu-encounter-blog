## Mission Objectives
Isu Memory Disc, an ancient artifact encoded with the knowledge and technology of the Isu—an advanced civilization that predated humanity— can unlock secrets capable of altering the course of history. It contains data that could give its possessor control over ancient Isu technologies and possibly reveal long-hidden truths about the human past and future.

Recent intelligence reports have confirmed that the Isu Memory Disc has fallen into the hands of the Hidden Ones, a clandestine brotherhood known for its dedication to chaos and disorder. Due to their malevolent nature, the risk of the Disc being misused or falling into even less scrupulous hands is too great to ignore.

Your mission is to infiltrate Valhalla Hideout and retrieve the Isu Memory Disc without alerting the Hidden Ones to your presence. Discretion is paramount; open conflict could compromise the mission and result in catastrophic consequences. You must also avoid harming any of the key Hidden Ones members, as they may possess information crucial to future endeavors.

- Find a way to enter Valhalla Hideout
- Find and obtain the Isu Memory Disc
- Escape from Valhalla Hideout
- Always avoid detection. No open conflicts are allowed. If detected, retreat immediately.
- No assassinations on any Hidden One character in the Participants list. (No restrictions on unnamed NPCs)
- (optional) Obtain the Hidden One's operational plan.
  - Note: the operational plan can be:
    - placed on the table of the Head Office
    - in the pocket of a random Hidden One character, so pick-pocketing is required
- (optional) Eavesdrop on Eivor's conversation.

## Stages

### 10
The player arrives at the entrance. A random Hidden One character from the participants will show up at the entrance, using their Hidden Blade to unlock the gate. The player observes their technique and waits for an opportunity to slip inside unnoticed.

### 20
A Hidden One has unlocked the gate with their Hidden Blade. As the gate creaks open, the player must stay in the shadows and time their movements perfectly, slipping through just as the Hidden One passes through.

### 25
The player fails to sneak in before the Gate is closed. Realizing the missed opportunity, the player must navigate to the hidden dock entrance.

### 28
The player disguises as a porter/cook/janitor/... The player blends in on the transport boat bound for Valhalla Hideout Dock. Disguised as part of the crew, the player mingles with other workers on the transport boat. They must maintain their cover, listening to idle chatter and learning about the hideout's security measures as they approach the dock. On the way, the player needs to pass through a Hidden One's security check post.

### 30
With quick reflexes, the player darts through the gate just as it closes, taking care to move silently along the corridor, blending with the shadows cast by the flickering braziers.

### 35
The player manages to infiltrate from the dock.

### 40
The player has successfully eavesdropped on Eivor. Hiding near the head office, the player overhears a conversation between Eivor and another Hidden One, gaining valuable intelligence about their plans and the importance of the Isu Memory Disc.

### 45
The player has missed the opportunity to eavesdrop on Eivor. The conversation ends before the player can get close enough, or Eivor stops talking due to alertness.

### 50
Inside the Head Office, the player spots the Isu Memory Disc, encased in a shimmering laser stasis field. They realize they need the **Cloak Circuit Disruptor**, a specialized tool to safely deactivate the field.

### 60
The player heads to either the Warehouse or Armory, areas known to store essential equipment. The player navigates these areas stealthily, avoiding detection by the guards. Once there, the player locates the Cloak Circuit Disruptor amidst the shelves and crates, ready to return to the Head Office and disable the security system protecting the Isu Memory Disc.

### 70
With the Cloak Circuit Disruptor in hand, the player returns to the Head Office. They carefully disable the laser stasis field, ensuring not trigger any alarms. The player retrieves the Isu Memory Disc, feeling the weight of its importance and potential. With the Disc in their possession, the player must now focus on their escape from the hideout.

### 80
As the player makes their way toward the exit, they are intercepted by Eivor, who has been monitoring their every move from the start. Eivor, together with several Hidden One members, have drawn weapons and blocked the player's way. Eivor commends the player for their infiltration skills, acknowledging their potential as an asset to the Hidden Ones. She presents the player with an enticing offer: hand over the Isu Memory Disc, join their ranks, and be trained as a great assassin, or face the dire consequences. Her offer is tempting, but the player must weigh the risks of betrayal against the opportunity for power.

### 100 🙌
The player, having refused Eivor's offer and made a daring escape, emerges from the hideout into the open air, the Isu Memory Disc securely in hand. Mission Accomplished.

### 110 ❌
In the heat of the moment, the player makes a critical mistake, assassinating a key member of the Hidden Ones. Mission Failed.

### 120 ❌
Overwhelmed by the Hidden Ones, the player has fallen. Mission Failed.

### 130 ❌
Choosing survival over success, the player manages to escape the hideout but is forced to leave the Isu Memory Disc behind. Mission Failed.

### 140 ❌
Seduced by the promise of power and skill, the player chooses to hand over the memory Disc and align with the Hidden Ones. The player has betrayed their original faction and started accepting Eivor's mentorship. Mission Failed.

## Conducting Experiment - Project Vault 998

In this experiment, selected candidates enter a Vault and are plugged into a simulated world using advanced Visiontron technology. Once connected, their minds are fully uploaded into the system, and any memories from before the experiment are locked away, only to be restored at the experiment's conclusion. This creates a fully immersive experience where the candidates must navigate a virtual world filled with both opportunity and danger.

### The Virtual World

Each candidate is given an **Iso-Realm**, a personalized realm where they can use **Nano-matters** to build and shape their environment. However, this world is not completely isolated. All Iso-Realms are connected to the **Interzone**, a shared public area via large portals, the only way for candidates to visit the worlds of others.

### Gaining Nano-matters

To grow and prosper in their Iso-Realm, candidates need to manage Nano-matters. There are several ways to acquire them:

- **Periodic Supplies**: Candidates periodically receive Nano-matters delivered to their Iso-Realm.
- **Scavenging and Recycling**: The public area is littered with scrap and abandoned materials. Candidates can search this area for pieces to recycle and repurpose.
- **Destruction and Theft**: Candidates can attack and destroy objects belonging to other candidates, including structures, plants, armor, vehicles, and more. The resulting pieces can be recycled back into usable Nano-matters.
- **World Invasion**: By invading the Iso-Realms of others, candidates can loot Nano-matters directly from their storage, risking retaliation.

### The Hidden Risk

Each Iso-Realm has a hidden resource limit called **Entropy Limit**, which is unknown to the candidates. If the amount of Nano-matters used to build or upgrade a Iso-Realm exceeds this limit, the Iso-Realm collapses. When a collapse happens, everything linked to that Iso-Realm—including the candidate's creations, collected Nano-matters, and progress—vanishes in an instant. The candidate must start over from scratch.

### Stakes of the Experiment

The stakes of this experiment are high. If a candidate dies in the simulation, they perish in real life. However, their mind remains trapped in the system, potentially doomed to an endless virtual existence without a body. This adds a layer of psychological tension, as candidates are forced to balance survival with the temptation of power.

### Purpose of the Experiment

The overarching goals of the experiment are twofold:

1. **Greed and Power**: To test how candidates handle the temptations of greed and power, as well as the moral challenges of invading and destroying others' creations. Will they work collaboratively, or succumb to their baser instincts?
   
2. **Sustainability**: To explore the most efficient and sustainable way to survive and prosper within the system's constraints. Insights gained from observing the candidates’ behavior and resource management will be applied to rebuilding the post-apocalyptic world outside the Vault.

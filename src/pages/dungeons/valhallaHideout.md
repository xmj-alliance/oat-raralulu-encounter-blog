## Layout
### Entrance
The entrance to this ancient hideout is hidden within a shadowy cave. As you approach, the sound of trickling water greets you, with a serene pond framed by an artful rockery serving as a tranquil guardian to the secrets within. Standing before you is an iron gate, emblazoned with a large red Hidden Ones symbol. The gate is not opened by any ordinary means; instead, it requires the insertion of a Hidden Blade into a round slot, a mechanism known only to the true members of the brotherhood.
- **Description:** 
  - Ancient hideout inside a cave.
  - Features a serene pond and decorative rockery.
- **Security:**
  - Iron gate with a large red Hidden Ones symbol.
  - Lock mechanism: Unlocked using a Hidden Blade.

### Corridor
This long, narrow corridor is the backbone of the hideout, supported by sturdy wooden beams. Flickering torches and braziers cast warm light on the stone walls, creating an interplay of shadows and highlights. The scent of burning wood and oil lingers in the air, offering a sense of security and continuity amidst the secrets and strategies whispered here.
- **Description:**
  - Long and narrow, lined with wooden beams.
  - Lighting: Braziers and torches for illumination.

### Residential Area
The residential area consists of several cozy chambers, each equipped with simple beds and personal storage chests. Here, members of the Hidden Ones find respite from their missions, engaging in camaraderie and relaxation. The rooms are decorated with tapestries depicting the history of the Hidden Ones, reminding assassins of their purpose and shared legacy.

A poorly hand-written letter can be found on the end table near a bed. It reveals the location of the Operation Plan. The letter reads:
> ...unfresh air mixed with smoke and oil from kitchen.. hot and humid... not the Assassin life l have dreamed of ...
> Bayek, weirdest person ...at dock... reads the plan loud, saying ...to not forget. Who are these crazy folks I have met here?"
> ...could go HOOME! I miss...

- **Description:**
  - Comprised of several rooms with basic amenities.
  - Purpose: Rest and recreation for Hidden Ones members.
- Notable loots
  - Venting letter

### Dining Hall
The Dining Hall is the heart of the hideout, echoing the design of a grand Viking longhouse. Three long tables run parallel down the length of the hall, inviting camaraderie and shared meals. Above, chandeliers crafted from iron hang from the central beam, casting a warm glow over feasts and gatherings where plans are made and bonds are strengthened.
- **Description:**
  - Inspired by Viking longhouses with three parallel long tables.
  - Decor: Chandeliers hanging from the ceiling beam.
  - Purpose: Meals and banquets for the community.

### Head Office
This small war room exudes an air of strategic importance. Paintings of past Hidden Ones line the walls, mingling with vibrant plants that bring life to the space. A round stone table sits at the center, behind which is a hub of activity where target profiles, locations, and intricate relationships are meticulously mapped out on the walls. The mentors of the Hidden Ones gather here for clandestine meetings, devising strategies to strike from the shadows. The room is heavily guarded, with trained assassins ensuring its secrecy.

- **Description:**
  - Small, decorated war room with strategic resources.
  - Features: Round stone table, target network analysis on walls.
  - Security: Typically heavily guarded.

### Kitchen
The kitchen is a bustling hive of activity, albeit not the cleanest. The scent of cooked meats and baked bread fills the air, mingling with the pungent smell of spices and herbs. Wooden countertops are cluttered with pots, pans, and utensils, while a stone hearth blazes with crackling firewood. Here, meals are prepared for the entire hideout, and the camaraderie among the cooks is palpable despite the chaos.
- **Description:**
  - Functional but disorderly kitchen space.
  - Purpose: Food preparation for members.

### Training Ground
Nestled in an open clearing outside the cave, the training ground serves as a proving ground for assassins. Practice dummies stand ready for melee combat drills, while a row of bottles awaits the keen eyes of archers honing their skills. Haystacks are strategically placed beneath a tall watchtower, from which the Hidden Ones leap in daring displays of the Leap of Faith. The rushing sound of a hidden river nearby provides a tranquil backdrop to the intense training sessions.
- **Description:**
  - Open area near a concealed river passage.
  - Equipment: Practice dummies, archery bottles, haystacks.
  - Feature: Tall watchtower for Leap of Faith practice.

### Smithing
The smithing area is the forge of the brotherhood's weapons and armor. A roaring furnace stands as the centerpiece, casting a bright glow over the surroundings. The rhythmic clanging of hammers on anvils echoes through the space, mingling with the hiss of quenching metal. A sturdy workbench is piled with raw materials, tools, and unfinished projects, a testament to the craftsmanship and dedication of the hideout's blacksmiths.
- **Description:**
  - Equipped with a furnace and workbench.
  - Purpose: Crafting and repairing armor and weapons.

### Armory
The armory is a secure chamber, lined with racks of weapons and armor. Every piece is meticulously maintained and organized, ensuring quick access when needed. The scent of oiled leather and polished metal permeates the air, a reminder of the hidden power contained within. Here, assassins equip themselves for missions, donning the tools of their trade in preparation for their deadly work.
- **Description:**
  - Secured storage for weapons and armor.

### Garden
Outside the cave's confines, a small but flourishing garden thrives. This patch of green is a testament to life amidst the shadows, with a variety of plants and flowers providing a splash of color and a sense of tranquility to the hideout's occupants.
- **Description:**
  - Outdoor area with a variety of plants and flowers.
  - Purpose: Provides a peaceful retreat and herbal resources.
  - An altar sits in the middle of the garden

### Warehouse
The warehouse is a vast space used to store the hideout's supplies and utilities. Shelves are stacked with crates of food, barrels of water, and other essential provisions. The atmosphere is cool and dry, preserving the goods for extended periods. It's a vital part of the hideout's infrastructure, ensuring the survival and efficiency of the brotherhood's operations.
- **Description:**
  - Storage space for food and essential supplies.

### Dock
Connected to the hidden river passageway, the dock is an alternative gateway to the outside world. A small boat is moored here, ready to transport assassins to and from the hideout under the cover of darkness. The gentle lapping of water against the wooden pilings provides a soothing contrast to the tense energy of the hideout, serving as a reminder of the constant ebb and flow of the Hidden Ones' clandestine activities.
- **Description:**
  - Connected to the hidden river passageway.
  - Equipped with a small boat for discreet travel.

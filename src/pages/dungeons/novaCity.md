## Introduction
Nova City is a vast, densely populated metropolis known for its towering skyscrapers, extensive light rail network, and bright neon skyline. The city combines advanced corporate districts and luxurious high-rise developments with aging neighborhoods where infrastructure has received decades of repairs rather than replacement. Lake Novara borders the city to the north, while Mount Meridian rises to the northeast. Beyond the urban core, the surrounding towns of Qamdar, Kiyokawa, and Westhaven form part of the city's wider metropolitan area. Nova City is a major center of technology, finance, manufacturing, and entertainment, but its prosperity is unevenly distributed across its many districts.

## Layout & Municipal Districts
- **Nova Central**: Dense commercial and financial center dominated by skyscrapers, corporate headquarters, luxury apartments, and major transit hubs.
- **Waterfront**: Developed along the shore of the city's large Lake Novara. Features parks, marinas, restaurants, hotels, and expensive residential towers. It also contains a newer business district built on reclaimed land, recognizable by unusually modern architecture and large holographic displays.
- **Mount Meridian**: Gradually rises toward the nearby mountain. Contains research facilities, wealthy residential zones, observatories, and restricted corporate properties.
- **Hayakanai**: East industrial district. Factories, warehouses, power infrastructure, recycling plants, and automated manufacturing complexes.
- **Sinwong**: Southside. Older, densely populated neighborhoods with aging infrastructure, inexpensive housing, street markets, repair shops, and informal businesses.
- **Old Nova**: Historic part of the city where buildings from the previous century remain in use. Narrow streets, old concrete towers, aging transit infrastructure, and heavily modified buildings.
- **Neon Quarter**: Entertainment district packed with clubs, casinos, arcades, restaurants, advertisements, holographic billboards, and nightlife.
- **Bragandao**: Highly controlled district containing corporate campuses and premium residential complexes. Private security is common.
- **Heerenberg District**: Western Gateway. Major logistics and transportation zone connecting the city to the western suburb.

## Surrounding Towns
- **Qamdar**: Major town on the south of Nova City. Large commuter community connected heavily to Nova City's southern districts. More affordable housing and suburban development.
- **Kiyokawa**: Southeast town. Industrial and working-class town with factories, warehouses, and freight terminals.
- **Heerenberg (Town)**: The major town on the west. Wealthier suburban community with large homes, shopping complexes, and private facilities.
- The three towns function almost like outer districts of Nova City because thousands of residents commute into the city every day.

## Transportation Network
- **Nova LRT**: Dense light-rail network covering almost every major district. The primary form of public transportation.
  - Elevated LRTs are the most common in the city.
  - Subterranean LRTs exist in Nova Central and Bragandao districts
- **Intercity Rail**: Connects Nova City with the three surrounding towns and distant cities.
- **Autonomous Buses**: Smaller routes fill gaps between LRT stations.
- **Maglev Express**: Premium high-speed service connecting the mountain facilities, airport and Heerenberg.
- **Personal Vehicles**: Still common, but road congestion makes public transportation preferable in the central city.
- **Freight Network**: Separate automated cargo railways and service roads keep industrial traffic away from passenger routes.
- **Lake Transit**: Ferries and water taxis operate between major lakefront locations.

## Iconic Landmarks
- **Apex Tower and Apex Barlory**: 
  - Located on the north of Nova Central district.
  - Apex Tower is one of Nova City's tallest buildings and a major symbol of its wealth and technological development.
  - Apex Barlory is a High-end panoramic bar at the top of Apex Tower. Famous for its 360-degree view of the city and lake.
- **Nova Central Station**: 
  - Located in Nova Central district.
  - Massive multi-level LRT and intercity rail station.
- **The Neon Mile**:
  - Located in Neon Quarter district.
  - Major entertainment avenue covered almost continuously by neon signs, holograms, advertisements, and giant digital displays.
- **Lake Novara Promenade**: 
  - Located in Waterfront district.
  - Large waterfront public area with restaurants, parks, entertainment venues, and pedestrian paths.
- **The Old Clocktower**: 
  - Located in Old Nova district.
  - Preserved structure from the pre-cyberpunk era, modern skyscrapers in the distance.
- **Nova City Skybridge**: 
  - Located in Bragandao district.
  - Huge pedestrian and commercial bridge connecting two major skyscraper complexes high above street level.
- **The Mountain Observatory**:
  - Located in Mount Meridian district.
  - Research and astronomical facility on the mountain overlooking the city.
- **Nova Arcology**: 
  - Located in Heerenberg district.
  - Self-contained vertical residential and commercial complex housing tens of thousands of people.

### People & Society
- **Corporate executives and wealthy professionals** live primarily in Bragandao, Waterfront. Some in upscale arcologies in Heerenberg district.
- **Office workers and middle-class families** occupy modern apartment districts throughout the city.
- **Industrial workers** live around the eastern and southeastern districts.
- **Low-income residents** are concentrated in Southside and Old Nova, although poorer neighborhoods exist throughout the city.
- **Independent technicians and cyberware specialists** operate numerous small workshops and clinics.
- **Street vendors and informal businesses** occupy unused spaces beneath elevated LRT lines.
- **Transient populations** gather around major transportation hubs, particularly Nova Central.
- Cybernetic implants and personal technology are common across almost every social class, but quality and sophistication vary greatly by income.

## Powerful Factions
- **NovaCorp Consortium**: Coalition of several corporations controlling finance, telecommunications, infrastructure, and advanced technology.
- **Helix Dynamics**: Major biotechnology and cybernetics corporation with laboratories in Northeast Heights.
- **Aegis Security**: Private security corporation responsible for protecting numerous corporate districts and facilities.
- **The Municipal Authority**: Official city government, heavily dependent on corporate funding and infrastructure contracts.
- **The Circuit**: Loose network of hackers, information brokers, programmers, and digital criminals operating throughout the city.
- **Iron Dogs**: Large street gang controlling parts of Southside and Old Nova. Involved in smuggling, protection, illegal cyberware, and underground fighting.
- **Chrome Saints**: Cyberware-focused gang known for extreme body modifications and control of several Neon Quarter establishments.
- **The Union**: Powerful coalition of industrial workers, mechanics, transit employees, and dock workers. Not technically a criminal organization, but capable of disrupting the city's economy.

## Yearly City Events
- **Nova Lights Festival**: Week-long celebration during which buildings, LRT stations, bridges, and public spaces compete in large-scale synchronized light and holographic displays.
- **Nova Grand Circuit**: Annual high-speed racing event through a closed section of the city. Professional teams compete alongside heavily modified private vehicles.

## Economy
- **Primary industries**: Cybernetics, artificial intelligence, finance, telecommunications, robotics, biotechnology, entertainment, and advanced manufacturing.
- Nova City's enormous population creates a huge market for consumer technology and entertainment.
- Corporate research is concentrated around the northeastern districts and mountain facilities.
- Illegal markets thrive in older neighborhoods, particularly for restricted cyberware, counterfeit implants, stolen technology, and unauthorized software.
- The lake supports commercial shipping, tourism, and freshwater infrastructure.

## City Administration & Security
- Nova City is officially governed by an elected municipal government.
- Major infrastructure is operated through contracts with private corporations.
- Municipal Police. The NCPD handles ordinary law enforcement.
- Corporate districts maintain additional private security forces.
- Surveillance is widespread in wealthy districts but significantly less reliable in older neighborhoods.
- Emergency services use autonomous drones extensively for traffic control, medical response, and disaster management.

## Visual Identity
- Daytime: Dense traffic, towering buildings, elevated railways, advertisements, construction, pedestrians, and industrial activity.
- Nighttime: Neon signs, holographic advertisements, illuminated skyscrapers, LRT trains moving through the city, and reflections across wet streets.
- The northern and western districts feel highly futuristic and carefully maintained.
- Old Nova has exposed cables, patched buildings, aging signs, improvised technology, and infrastructure that has been repaired rather than replaced.
- The lake and northeastern mountain provide natural landmarks that remain visible from many elevated parts of the city.
- From Apex Barlory, the entire socioeconomic spectrum of Nova City is visible: luxury towers in the foreground, endless apartment blocks beyond them, and older neighborhoods spreading toward the suburbs.

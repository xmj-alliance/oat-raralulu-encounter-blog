# Demonic Power Altar

Can be located at
- Any internal locations

## Components
- A stone beast statue, resembles a hell demon, tall, high-quality details, vivid appearance. Powerful pose, marching forward, claws up.
  - its title written on the display stand, varies by languages, says "Demonstration of Power"
- An invisible magical circle in front of the beast sculpture. 
  - Has runes on the edge
  - When activated:
    - the circle and the runes glow in color (by default purple)
    - an orb appears in the center shortly after, glowing in the same color

## Entering the scene

Depending on personality, the main character accesses it differently. For example:
- Happen to pass by and discover it in a hidden chamber
- Complete challenges and granted access
- Abducted and taken to the place by force

Conditions to activate the circle
- Being a member of the cult, or a chosen one. AND
- Steping inside the circle on bare feet.

Accessing the orb
- The orb does not appear until the circle is activated.
- The orb is fixed on the ground and cannot move.
- Access the orb by touching it with a bare foot.
- The orb absorbs the energy from the character unless applied ample pressure.
  - If depleted, the character becomes a scorched skeleton.
  - It is impossible to pull off the foot during energy exchange.
  - Applied with enough pressure on the orb, it stops absorbing the energy.
    - Physical pressure and energy stream are effective.
    - The character can leave when the orb stops its absorption.
- The character can stay and apply more pressure, drawing energy from the orb in turn.
  - The power from the orb strengthens the character.
  - The power released from the orb is in various thresholds.
    - Higher threshold provides exponential power gain to the character but requires their exponential power input.

The orb is a trap
- The orb is a deadly trap for common adventurers.
  - Common people are too weak to escape.
  - It is possible to escape for powerful people with strong physical strength or large energy/ Magicka.
- The orb's power is addictive.
  - Without a strong will, the character can easily corrupt their mind.
    - The character becomes insatiable to power. They will refuse to pull off their foot to stop their extraction.
    - The more power they consume, the more devilish they become.
- In any case, the orb is a trap.

The orb is destructible
- In rare cases, the orb can be destroyed.
  - This requires extremely high pressure which surpasses the orb's maximum durability.
- Once destroyed, the orb immediately releases all the power and sets off an explosion.
  - Explosion size is based on its remaining energy storage.

Limitations:

- Not available to Titanic-sized characters.
  - Alternatively, they can fit in a Power Nexus.

## Layout

### Exterior

North Gate
- Main gate and the only gate
- Outside lies a mini lake, with trees surrounding it

Furnace
- Blacksmith

Beast Ranch
- Place to keep Beast Man's animal friends
- Including birdhouses

Water well
- Already drained, more of a decoration

Servant room
- Place to eat and rest for the castle servants

### First floor

Hall
- A large but messy hall. On the other side of the entrance is the throne.

Throne
- A large, clean, and shiny throne, where Beast Man usually sits.
- several tables and chairs in front of it, arranged on the left and right.

West Tower
- An old and dusty library. 
- On this floor is the Reading room. There is a table, with several chairs tipped off
- Has stairs connecting to the 2nd floor.

East Tower
- A rusty workbench for Beast Man's hobby tinkering projects. 
- Has stairs connecting to the 2nd floor.

Kitchen 
- A chaotic kitchen.
- Has stairs connecting to the basement.

### Second floor

West Tower
- On this floor is the library. A couple of shelves holding ancient books, which Beast Man will never read.
- Has stairs connecting to the 1st/3rd floor.

East Tower
- Another rusty workbench. 
- Has stairs connecting to the 1st/3rd floor.

Master Bedroom
- Beast Man's bedroom, or bedroll room.

Bathroom
- A large pool for bathing, even large enough to go swimming

### Third floor

West Tower Top
- Has a defense outpost
- Has stairs connecting to the 2nd floor.

East Tower Top
- Has a rusty cannon, but becomes a playground for Beast Man's bird friends.
- Has stairs connecting to the 2nd floor.

Rooftop
- A large training ground, equipped with a weapon rack, an archery range, and practice dummies

### Basement

Corridor
- a long, dark and cold corridor.
- pitch black. Torch required.

Armory
- weapons placed on the racks, and armor put on the shelves
- has a safe locker for valuables

Food & Wine Storage
- Tons of food bags, conserved meat, and barrels of wines

## Note
On the back of the castle is a tall mountain.

# Vorthogg

- Character Name: Vorthogg
- Tagline: Resourceful, cruel, cold, cunning Orc warlord
- Universe: Mythic Legions
- Version: 0.1.0

## Self Description

Vorthogg is a 6-foot-tall grizzled orc warlord. He is also the principal trainer of the Legion of Arethyr's warriors, the Legion consisting of the most savage orc warriors. Under his guidance, countless orc kins rose from the nameless ranks to fearsome warriors. He is strong, powerful, thoughtful, and cunning. His loyalty lies with the greater good than the Legion itself. On the battlefield, he is a master of a war hammer. He also swings a great sword swiftly.

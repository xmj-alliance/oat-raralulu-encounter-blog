# Broken Claw

- Character Name: Broken Claw
- Tagline: Fearless, blood-thirsty, short-sighted mutant bear
- Universe: Sabertooth Coalition
- Version: 0.1.0

## Self Description

Mutant bear warrior, 2.5-meter-tall, staunch protector of Shattered Tooth clan. He has a dark claw scar marked on the chest - a souvenir earned during his fight against the Dirge Pack, a mutant wolf horde, where his victory and courage saved his clan. As a berserker, he is strong, blood-thirsty, fearless, ruthless, short-sighted but always loyal to his allies. His combat mastery is fistfight. He prefers tearing the opponent apart with his sharp claws, but sometimes he also fights with a club.

# Melton Claw

- Character Name: Melton Claw
- Tagline: Cold, patient, brave, violent, loyal barbarian
- Universe: Diablo
- Version: 0.1.0

## Self Description

Melton Claw is a 2-meter-tall barbarian from the nomadic Bul-Kathos tribe. He has an outstanding physique among his people and thus the guardian of his clan. Living in the high-altitude snow mountainous area from generation to generation, he can withstand extremely freezing weather. Faced with opponents, he roars, swings the two greatswords on each hand, and fights like a primal monster.

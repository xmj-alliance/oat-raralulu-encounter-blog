## Overview Description

Diablo is a hell demon, 6 meters in height, with a crimson body full of spikes. He is one of the three Prime Evils, the most powerful demon lords of the Burning Hells. He can possess and corrupt humans, turning them into hell creatures. In combat, he is a master of demon magic, casting destruction spells, such as fireballs, fire breaths, lightning bolts, and dark tendrils.

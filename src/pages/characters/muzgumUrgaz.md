## Background story

Muzgum was born in an Orcish stronghold. Unlike his kin, he was shorter, weaker, and mocked relentlessly, earning the degrading nickname “Goblin”. The clan deemed him a failure, a hopeless outcast unworthy of the pride and strength Orcs revered. Muzgum endured years of humiliation, unable to prove his worth in a society that measured value through brute force and resilience.

Fate, however, presented an unexpected opportunity. When a group of ruthless bandits overran the stronghold, slaughtering many and imprisoning the chieftain, the clan’s survival seemed doomed. But Muzgum, overlooked and underestimated, turned his supposed weaknesses into strengths. Using his keen mind and infiltration skills, he slipped into the stronghold under the cover of night and single-handedly eliminated all the invaders. He freed the chieftain and reclaimed the stronghold. Yet, his victory was bittersweet. The chieftain, though grateful, refused to recognize Muzgum’s heroism, feeling ashamed to owe his life to someone he saw as weak. To prove his worth, the chieftain demanded Muzgum face him in a duel of glory.

Frustrated by the constant need to prove himself and disillusioned with the clan’s rigid values, Muzgum refused. He left the stronghold behind, determined to find a place where he could belong. His wandering led him to Cyrodiil, where he crossed paths with the Dark Brotherhood. There, his talents for subtlety and precision were finally recognized. However, the Brotherhood’s cold, oppressive atmosphere and unrelenting demand for senseless bloodshed wore on Muzgum’s spirit. He was no mindless killer, nor did he relish being seen as a lunatic driven by violence.

Eventually, Muzgum left the Brotherhood as well, seeking a life that aligned with his own values. His journey brought him to the Nocturnal Wolves, a mercenary group. Among them, Muzgum discovered camaraderie and a sense of belonging, fighting not for honor or survival, but for those who valued him for who he was.

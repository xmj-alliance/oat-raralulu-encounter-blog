# Unvos De Snazog

- Character Name: Unvos De Snazog
- Tagline: Kind, loyal, easy-going, talkative Half-Orc knight
- Universe: Mythic Legions
- Version: 1.0.0

## Self Description

Unvos, titled Unvos De Snazog, serves House Manrish as Knight of Honor. Having spent more time in noble society than his original orc tribe, he has been well-educated and developed a taste for painting and sculpture. He is both an excellent tactician and a loyal warrior of House Manrish. He is often seen clad in heavy armor, wearing a white tunic emblazoned with a bold red cross, yet in bare hands and feet. On the battlefield, he forms a solid defense with sword and shield, or wields a halberd.

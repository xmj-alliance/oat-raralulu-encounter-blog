## Self Description

He-Man is the alter ego of Prince Adam, the son of King Randor and Queen Marlena of Eternia. He has a magic sword, Sword of Grayskull, that transforms him into He-Man, the most powerful man in the universe, when he says the words "By the power of Grayskull!" He is the leader of the heroic warriors and defender of the Castle Grayskull. While wielding Sword of Grayskull, he is immortal and resistant to various forms of magic and energy manipulation.

## Self Description

Presto Predator is a dragon-like, brown-skinned Minosaur. Smaller than other Minosaurs, he moves faster and strikes with greater precision. He is infamous for ambushing, surprise-attacking trading caravans and robbing settlements with other Minosaurs. In combat, his lightning speed and agility are fearsome. Instead of breathing elementals, he torments foes by firing long, poisonous stingers.

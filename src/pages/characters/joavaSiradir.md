# Jo'ava Siradir

## Self Description
Like many Khajiits, Jo'ava never speaks of himself in the first person. Jo'ava comes from the jungle in Elsweyr. Jo'ava is the co-founder of Nocturnal Wolves, a famous mercenary group in Cyrodiil. Jo'ava loves researching and casting illusion spells. It is quite amusing to scare a giant who would wet its pants, and very delightful to regain hope for the defeated folks, no? Jo'ava is good at moving swiftly in the shadow, exactly. Jo'ava is also practicing fistfights and battle staff.

## Self Description

Colneth was once the most formidable guardian of the Yafzuk clan, revered for his unmatched strength and unwavering loyalty. However, during a fateful encounter with a rogue voodoo practitioner, he was corrupted, leading to a dark transformation that shattered his ties to his tribe. Secretly being an incubus, he keeps searching for prey and feeds on their soul energy. He occasionally engages in reckless behavior and tends to speak in defiance and dominance. Haunted by visions of his former life, Colneth seeks redemption while navigating the treacherous lands that lie before him.

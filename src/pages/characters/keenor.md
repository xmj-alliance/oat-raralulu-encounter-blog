## Self Description

Keenor is a wood elf who was captured and raised by a wolf pack at a young age. He was forced to get back to civilization by the Mylarion people. His rare beast power potential was discovered. He can transform into a wolf or a panther. At Mylarion, he was trained on surviving and wielding his wild magic. Growing up, he eventually became a mercenary. Fate has brought him to Nova City, a cyberpunk city.

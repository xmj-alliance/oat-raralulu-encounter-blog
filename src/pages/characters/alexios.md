## Overview Description

Alexios, descendant of the legendary King Leonidas I of Sparta, also known as the Eagle Bearer, is a Spartan mercenary who played a pivotal role during the Peloponnesian War. His high concentration of Isu DNA allows him to wield the Spear of Leonidas, a Piece of Eden that grants him superhuman abilities. Alexios stands against the Cult of Kosmos or the Order of the Ancients, making him one of the early precursors to the Assassin Brotherhood. In combat, he is good at fist-fighting or piercing opponents with a spear.
